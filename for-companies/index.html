// What we handle

The dispute work, done for you

Hand over as much or as little as you like: new-client audits, ongoing rounds, or both.

Credit report audits

We go through each new client’s three reports and list every item worth disputing, so your onboarding is fast.

Dispute letters, every round

Round-by-round letters to the bureaus, creditors, and collectors, in your style and on your letterhead.

CFPB complaints & escalations

When bureaus stall or verify without proof, we prepare the next step: escalations, method-of-verification requests, and CFPB complaints.

Client file updates

After each round we import the results, update the client’s file, and note what’s next, so your team can update clients.

Work in your software

We log in to the CRM you already use and follow your process. No new tools for you or your clients.

Under your brand

Your clients only ever see your company. We stay in the background.
