// Performance

Everyone’s performance on one page

Pick Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days or a month. For each person: days worked, hours, average start time, activity, productive time, tasks done and a score out of 100.

  • A “needs attention” list written for you, like “low activity (29%)”
  • Click a name to open that person’s day
  • Colour bars make it easy to spot who is doing well
// Proof of work

Activity, screenshots, apps & websites

Every 10 minutes: how active the person was (keyboard and mouse), a screenshot if you turn them on, and the main app or website in use.

  • You rate apps and sites as productive, neutral or unproductive
  • Only website names are kept, never full addresses
  • Screenshots and app tracking can be switched off company-wide
Team records: fix a forgotten clock-out in seconds. Every change is in the audit log.

  • Every change records who made it and when

// Questions

Frequently asked

What happens after the 30-day trial?

If you like it, you pay the $200 setup once and keep using everything, with all your trial data. Support is $50 a year. If not, we simply close your account. No card is needed to start.

Do I need my own server or database?

No. Smart Clock is hosted for you on secure cloud servers that we set up and look after, including backups.

Is there a limit on staff?

The price is for small and growing teams. For more than 20 staff, we confirm the price with you before your trial ends, so there are no surprises.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. There are Windows and Mac desktop apps, plus a web version that opens in any browser. Updates install automatically.

Is it legal to monitor staff?

Smart Clock is designed to be open: staff agree before tracking starts, it only runs while they are clocked in, and they can see their own activity. Rules differ by country, so check what applies to your business.

Can you change it for my business?

Yes. Small changes are part of support. Bigger features or a custom version are quoted separately after a quick chat.
