// What we dispute

Items we can challenge

If something on your report may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable, you have the right to dispute it.

Late payments

Late marks reported with the wrong dates, amounts, or status.

Collections

Collection accounts that are duplicated, outdated, or can’t be verified.

Charge-offs

Charged-off accounts with incorrect balances or reporting history.

Hard inquiries

Inquiries you don’t recognise or didn’t authorise.

Repossessions & public records

Repossessions, judgments, and bankruptcies reported inaccurately.

Personal information

Wrong names, old addresses, and employers that clutter your file.

// How it works

From review to results

Most files run in dispute rounds of about 30 to 45 days, the time bureaus usually take to respond.

1

Free credit review

Share your goals and your credit reports. We go through all three bureaus line by line.

2

Your action plan

You get a clear list of what we’ll dispute, what we need from you, and realistic timelines.

3

Dispute rounds

We prepare and send disputes to the bureaus and creditors, round by round, and track every response.

4

Weekly updates

You hear from us every week with what changed, what’s next, and how to keep your credit healthy.

What we need from you

  • Your current credit reports from all three bureaus (we’ll show you how to get them)
  • A photo ID and proof of address, for the bureaus to confirm it’s you
  • Any letters you receive from bureaus or creditors during the process

Please don’t email your Social Security number. We’ll share a secure way to send documents.

Our honest approach

We don’t guarantee score increases or removals, and we never dispute information we know is accurate. We don’t provide legal advice. What we do promise is a documented, lawful process and clear communication.
