// Questions

What agencies ask us

Which software do you work in?

Whatever you already use. We log in as members of your team and follow your process, templates and letter style.

Will my clients know you exist?

No. Everything goes out under your company’s name. We never contact your clients unless you ask us to.

How fast is the turnaround?

Usually 24 to 48 hours per task. We’re based in India, so much of the work happens overnight US time and is ready when your team starts the day.

How does the free trial work?

You share a few files, we do the work, and you check it. If you like it, we agree on volume and pricing. If not, you’ve lost nothing.

How is pricing worked out?

Per file or as a monthly package, depending on your volume. Tell us roughly how many files you handle and we’ll send rates.

How do you keep client data safe?

We work inside your system with logins you control and can remove at any time. We don’t keep copies of client documents, and we’re happy to sign your NDA.

Is there a minimum or a contract?

No long contract to start. Begin with the trial, then grow at your own pace.
