Questions

Good to know.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

If you like it, you pay the $200 setup once and keep everything, including your trial data. Support and hosting is $50 a year. If not, we simply close your account. No card is needed to start.

Do I need my own server or IT person?

No. Smart Clock is hosted for you on secure cloud servers that we set up and look after, including backups.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. There are Windows and Mac desktop apps, plus a web version that opens in any browser. Updates install automatically.

Is there a limit on staff?

The price is for small and growing teams. For more than 20 staff, we confirm the price with you before your trial ends, so there are no surprises.

Is it legal to monitor staff?

Smart Clock is built to be open: staff agree before tracking starts, it only runs while they're clocked in, and they can see their own activity. Rules differ by country and state, so check what applies to your business.

Can you change it for my business?

Yes. Small changes are part of support. Bigger features or a custom version are quoted separately after a quick chat.
